OutpatientController.ts Description:

1. Patient Validation & Registration: 
   - Ensures a patient exists in the master `Patient` table before allowing the creation of a new outpatient record.
   - Links the visit effectively to their NIC.

2. Daily Operations & Filtering: 
   - Provides endpoints to fetch outpatient records created specifically "today" (`getAllOutPatientsToday`).
   - Counts today's visits (`getNumberOfOutPatientsToday`) to facilitate daily operational dashboards.

3. History & Staffing: 
   - Retrieves full outpatient history for a specific patient via their NIC (`getOutPatientByNic`).
   - Fetches the list of staff members currently assigned to the "Outpatient" ward (`getStaffMemebers`).
